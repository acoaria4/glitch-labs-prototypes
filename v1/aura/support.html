Support

Need help with AURA? Tell us what you were doing and what happened. We can help with charts, saved profiles, the calendar, or accessing the beta.

Email AURA support ↗

Report a problem

Include your device model, operating system, app version, and the steps that led to the issue. If a screenshot helps, remove names and birth details before sharing it.

You can also report an issue on GitHub. Keep personal information out of public reports.

Check a chart or location

Review the date, time, and place of birth you entered. If a place is not in the offline database, the app’s Privacy settings let you control optional online geocoding.

Manage profiles and privacy

The in-app Privacy panel includes data export, prediction-cache clearing, saved-profile deletion, and controls for optional geocoding and citations. See Data controls for details.

Get the app

AURA’s beta is available on Google Play and TestFlight. Find both links on the download page.

Privacy questions

Read the Privacy Policy for how AURA processes birth details, chart information, and saved profiles. For a private question, contact us by email.
